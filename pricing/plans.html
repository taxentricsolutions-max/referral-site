Which tax return does your business file?

Not sure? Check last year's business return or ask us. If your business gives you a K-1 (the form showing your share of the profit), your personal return is included.

How many transactions does your business have each month?

A transaction is any money in or out, like a sale, bill payment, card purchase, or transfer. Not sure? Count the lines on last month's bank and card statements. Bookkeeping includes sorting your transactions, matching your books to your bank, and a monthly profit and loss report.
